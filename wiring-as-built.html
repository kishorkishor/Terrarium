AS-BUILT wiring bench state · 2026-08-26

One-to-one with what is physically on the desk and verified working — plus everything not yet connected, drawn dashed and listed unchecked. The full final build (12V chain, Board B, fuse, veroboard) stays in master-build-guide.html.
Click any wire (or its row below) to trace it end-to-end. Click empty space to reset. Checklist ticks save in this browser.
This bench build differs from the original plan on purpose: the MOSFET board's inputs need ~5 V, so every PWM terminal sits on VIN (5 V) and the GND terminal is the signal the ESP32 pulls low (firmware MOSFET_ACTIVE_LOW 1). The 10k pulldowns from the old plan are removed. The 12V adapter plugs directly into the buck's barrel jack — fuse + rocker chain comes later with Board B.
