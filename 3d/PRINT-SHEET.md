## Material (transparent)

- Ask for **clear resin (SLA), polished or clear-coated** if the shop has a resin printer with a 200 mm build. This is the only printed option that is close to see-through.
- Otherwise **transparent / natural PETG**, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20-25 % infill, no supports. It comes out frosted: light passes, you see shapes, not detail.
- Do not use PLA (softens in warm humid air).
- Wall thickness is 2.4 mm. Bays print upright, open side up. Damper plate prints flat.

## Hardware

- M3 x 12 screws + nuts, 16 pcs (bay-to-bay flanges)
- M3 x 20 screw, 1 pc (holds damper open)
- 40 x 40 x 10 mm fan, 4 x M3 x 16
- Aquarium silicone for the inside seams of the terrarium bay
