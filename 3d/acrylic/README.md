| File | Material | Sheet |

| `sheet_B_3mm` | 3 mm clear **cast** acrylic | 900 x 600 mm |

If their bed is smaller than 900 x 600, they can re-nest the parts; every part is a closed shape.
The `.ai` files are PDF-format Illustrator files. Open in Illustrator and Save As if the shop wants a specific AI version.
`PART-MAP_sheet_*.pdf` shows which outline is which part. Do not send the part map for cutting.

## Assembly

1. Dry-fit first. Cross panels (2 ends, 2 dividers) tab into the front, back and base.
2. Glue with acrylic solvent cement (or chloroform from a sign shop). Run a bead of aquarium silicone along every inside seam of the terrarium bay and over the tab slots in its floor.
3. Damper: glue `guide_spacer` strips to the air-unit side of `divider_damper`, flush with the front and back walls, 5 mm above the floor. Drop the 2 mm plate between them, then glue `guide_cover` strips on top of the spacers so they overhang the plate. Plate resting on the floor = closed. Lift 10 mm and push an M3 screw through the lower tab hole so it rests on the lids = open.
4. Glue four `lid_locator` strips under each lid, just inside the walls, so lids cannot slide.
5. Keep the fittonias in pots or a plastic tray with drainage layer; do not fill the acrylic box itself with water.

## Onshape

- Quick look: import `acrylic-assembled.stl` (units: millimeter).
- Native, editable parts: import the files in `onshape-dxf-per-part/`, then in a Part Studio start a sketch, use **Insert DXF or DWG**, and extrude 3 mm (2 mm for the damper plate).
